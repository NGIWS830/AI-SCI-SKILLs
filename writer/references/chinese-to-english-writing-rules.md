# Chinese-to-English SCI Writing Rules

## Strategy

Translate meaning, not word order. Reorganize sentences when needed for natural English, but preserve scientific content and claim strength.

## Output options when useful

- Literal translation
- SCI-style rendering
- Notes on terminology or ambiguity

## Claim strength mapping

- “一定程度上提升” -> “improves to some extent” / “provides moderate improvements”
- “缓解” -> “alleviate” / “mitigate”, not “solve”
- “实验结果表明” -> “experimental results show/suggest”, not “prove”
- “具有潜力” -> “shows potential”, not “demonstrates superiority”

## Keep placeholders

- `[CITATION NEEDED]`
- `AUTHOR_INPUT_NEEDED`
