# Chinese Polishing Rules

## Goals

Improve academic clarity, coherence, paragraph logic, and technical precision.

## Preserve

- Scientific meaning
- Claim strength
- Metric values
- Dataset names
- Method names
- Citation placeholders

## Do not

- Add new experiments or conclusions
- Turn weak findings into strong claims
- Add unverified novelty
- Remove uncertainty markers

## Preferred revisions

- Replace口语化表达 with formal academic expression.
- Combine repetitive sentences.
- Clarify logical transitions.
- Keep technical terms consistent.
