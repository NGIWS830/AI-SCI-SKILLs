# Title Patterns

Good AI SCI titles are specific about task and method without overclaiming.

Patterns:

- `[Method Concept] for [Task]`
- `[Problem-Aware Module] for [Task] in [Domain]`
- `[Architecture/Framework Name]: [Core Mechanism] for [Task]`

Avoid titles that claim “novel”, “efficient”, or “robust” unless the paper strongly supports those claims.
